## A single guided tour

The [landing tour](/#try-it) follows a complete task in six chapters: requirements, evidence, selection and review. It uses the original narration and music, with synchronized subtitles and a compact comparison and result view.

Press **Play tour** to begin. Pause, mute, restart, scrub the timeline or select a chapter to explore at your own pace. The tour does not scroll the page or switch into a manual mode. You can follow every stage without connecting an agent.

## Try the working examples

Open [Examples](/examples) for hands-on flows. These use the same registry components and shared state as their WebMCP tools:

- [Shopping](/examples#comparison): compare machines and review a selection.
- [Project explorer](/examples#explorer): filter and select records.
- [Reservation](/examples#reservation): choose dates and validate a booking flow.
- [MCP workspace](/examples#console): connect to a remote server and inspect its tools.
- [Travel planner](/examples#travel): find a quiet stay within a budget.
- [Support inbox](/examples#support): filter tickets and review a resolution.
- [Workspace setup](/examples#onboarding): prepare and complete a validated setup.

Fictional examples do not place orders, create accounts or make reservations. Their ordinary controls work without WebMCP.

## Media and implementation

The video player has been removed from the landing. The original film, narration, music and subtitle assets are preserved. The tour uses an AAC stream copy of the original film, served with byte ranges from S3, and shares the film's timing and captions.

`video:render` still produces the film and extracts `demo-audio.m4a`. `PUBLISH_ASSET=demo-audio.m4a` publishes only the soundtrack. Changing the interface does not require regenerating the voice.
